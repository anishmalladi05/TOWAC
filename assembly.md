ASSEMBLY:

To assemble the paper version, please look in the assembly guide for the instructions, and get the printout from the ZIP file in the Other Files category. If you are making the 3D printed inserts, lay the pieces how you want them with no gaps, take one magnet, stick in in the hole, and which holding it in place with your finger, bring the other magnet into it's hole and let go of everything. The magnets should stay in place and hold each other, at which point you can slide in the insert. REMEMBER TO ONLY USE MAGNETS LESS THAN OR EQUAL TO 2MM X 7MM. For any questions on how this works, look at the paper assembly guide for the theory. If you still need help, DM me and put your question in the comments.
